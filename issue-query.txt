## 2. Required Features

Implement the following features using server-side Java and compatible Fabric APIs. Use Mixin only when necessary, with narrowly scoped injections.

1. **Sugar cane:** Allow placement on soul sand. When planted on soul sand, grow by one block approximately every second if growth conditions permit. On other valid vanilla substrates, preserve vanilla growth.
2. **Tree saplings:** All applicable vanilla saplings and propagules should grow into their correct trees within approximately 15 seconds under valid environmental and space conditions.
3. **Iron Block Villager:** A villager standing on an iron block becomes a special golem-spawning villager. Attempt to spawn exactly one iron golem every 100 server ticks (five seconds) whenever a valid spawn position exists. Do not require beds, village membership, gossip, panic, nearby mobs, professions, other villagers, or vanilla golem eligibility checks. Check only the iron-block condition and the physical validity of the spawn position. If no valid position exists, skip that attempt and retry at the next interval. Moving off the iron block disables special behavior; standing on one again reactivates it with a fresh five-second interval. Other villagers must retain vanilla behavior.
4. **Crops:** Accelerate applicable vanilla crop and plant growth to maturity within approximately 15 seconds under valid growth conditions. Preserve necessary soil, light, water, and plant-specific requirements.
5. **Animal breeding:** Remove the breeding cooldown for supported breedable vanilla animals, allowing repeated breeding whenever normal eligibility conditions are met. Baby animals should mature within approximately 15 seconds or instantly when fed once, according to the configured behavior.
6. **Sheep:** Allow sheep to regrow wool and become shearable approximately every five seconds, while preserving normal shearing drops and preventing duplicate drops.
7. **Villager emeralds:** Give villagers a 40% chance to drop one emerald on a clearly defined eligible event, preferably a successful trade. Evaluate the chance once per event and prevent duplicate rewards.
8. **Chicken eggs:** Every player-thrown chicken egg must spawn exactly one chicken, bypassing the vanilla random hatch chance without duplicate spawning.
9. **Chicken production:** Adult chickens produce one egg approximately every 20 seconds regardless of location or movement. Babies must not lay eggs.

## 3. Configuration

Implement a server-side configuration at `config/coreservermod.json`, generated automatically on first launch. Provide safe defaults, validation, and fallback behavior.

Support toggles and adjustable intervals/chances for each feature, including:

- `sugar_cane_soul_sand_enabled`
- `sugar_cane_growth_interval_seconds`
- `sapling_growth_time_seconds`
- `crop_growth_time_seconds`
- `iron_block_villager_enabled`
- `iron_golem_spawn_interval_seconds`
- `animal_breeding_cooldown_disabled`
- `baby_growth_time_seconds`
- `instant_growth_when_fed`
- `sheep_wool_regrowth_seconds`
- `villager_emerald_drop_chance`
- `guaranteed_chicken_from_egg`
- `chicken_egg_interval_seconds`

Optional additional tweaks may include faster smelting, automatic crop replanting, and selected mob quality-of-life features. Keep optional features disabled by default and avoid unbounded item or entity generation.

## 6. Testing

Test the following:

- Soul sand placement and one-second sugar cane growth.
- Tree and crop growth timing and valid environmental conditions.
- Iron Block Villager spawning without vanilla village requirements.
- Iron golem spawn-position collision checks and five-second intervals.
- Special spawning stopping off iron blocks and restarting when returning.
- Ordinary villagers retaining vanilla golem spawning behavior.
- Animal breeding, baby growth, sheep regrowth, and shearing drops.
- Emerald reward probability and event deduplication.
- Guaranteed chicken spawning from thrown eggs without duplicates.
- Adult chicken egg production and timing.
- Configuration generation, validation, and feature toggles.
- Successful mod loading, server startup, clean shutdown, and absence of recurring exceptions.

Minecraft normally runs at 20 ticks per second: 1 second = 20 ticks, 5 seconds = 100 ticks, 15 seconds = 300 ticks, and 20 seconds = 400 ticks. Actual elapsed time may increase under server lag.

Use automated tests wherever possible and controlled integration tests for actual Minecraft behavior. Do not interpret a small random sample as proof that a 40% probability is incorrect.
